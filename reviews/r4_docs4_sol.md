1. **ERROR** — “*the optimal schedule set by a trade-off between the LZ exponent and the number of Bloch periods consumed.*”  
   With fixed total momentum transfer \(Nk\), the number of Bloch periods/crossings \(N\) is fixed; it is not an optimization variable. The trade-off is between time spent near crossings and time spent between them.

2. **WINCE** — “*gap \(A_b\) (the plane's own Fourier amplitude*”  
   For \(A_b\cos(b\!\cdot\!x)\), the complex Fourier coefficients are \(A_b/2\), while the avoided-crossing gap is \(A_b\). Calling \(A_b\) the “Fourier amplitude” invites precisely the factor-of-two error that the subsequent exponent is sensitive to. Say “cosine amplitude” or “twice the complex Fourier coefficient.”

3. **WINCE** — “*within the fixed quasimomentum sector (his \texttt{SFrame})*”  
   This is being inserted into Swan’s manuscript, so “his” breaks authorial voice. Use “the code’s \texttt{SFrame} convention” or simply “\texttt{SFrame}.”
